While vanilla Minecraft clients can connect directly, these Fabric client mods provide dramatically higher FPS, shaders compatibility, and 3D positional voice chat.

Simple Voice Chat

Voice Chat

Adds proximity voice chat to Minecraft. Listen to other players in 3D audio as you move closer, with volume roll-off, occlusion through walls, and push-to-talk.

Sodium (Embeddium)

Performance

A free and open-source rendering engine replacement for the Minecraft client that greatly improves framerates, reduces micro-stutter, and fixes graphical issues.

Recommended Get Mod

Visuals

Modern shaderpack compatibility for Fabric 26.3 that runs at incredible framerates alongside Sodium. Enjoy vibrant sunrises, realistic water, and soft shadows.

Quick Connection Guide

How to Join NezoSMP

Choose your favorite Minecraft launcher below for simple step-by-step connection instructions.

01

Search Modpack

Open Modrinth or Prism Launcher, click Create Instance or Add Modpack, and search for NezoSMP.

02

1-Click Install

Click Install. The launcher will automatically configure Fabric 26.3, Sodium, and Simple Voice Chat for you.

03

Launch & Connect

Launch the game, open Multiplayer, and join play.nezosmp.net. You're ready to explore!

01

Create Profile

In the CurseForge App, click Create Custom Profile. Select Minecraft Version 26.3 and Modloader Fabric.

02

Add Voice Chat & Sodium

Click "Add More Content" and install Simple Voice Chat and Sodium.

03

Connect to Server

Hit Play, go to Multiplayer, add server with address play.nezosmp.net and join!

01

Install Fabric Loader

Visit fabricmc.net and download the Fabric installer for version 26.3.

02

Place Mods in .minecraft

Drop Fabric API and Simple Voice Chat into your .minecraft/mods folder.

03

Direct Connect

Open the Fabric profile in the official launcher, select Multiplayer, and join play.nezosmp.net.

01

Add External Server

In Bedrock Edition (Windows 10/11, Mobile, iOS, Android), go to the Servers tab and click Add Server.

02

Server Address & Port

Server Name: NezoSMP
Server Address: bedrock.nezosmp.net
Port: 19132 (Default).

03

Cross-Play Ready

Click Save and Join! You will cross-play seamlessly with Java Edition players thanks to GeyserMC.

Community Standards

Rules & Guidelines

We prioritize a drama-free, respectful, and friendly atmosphere for all players. Please review our server code of conduct below.

Respect other players' builds, chests, storage systems, and animal farms. Even unlocked or wilderness chests are strictly protected. Every block interaction is permanently logged with CoreProtect, allowing staff to roll back damage in seconds.
Only quality-of-life and performance mods (Sodium, Iris Shaders, Simple Voice Chat, AppleSkin, Minimap without cave-view) are permitted. X-Ray texture packs, hacked clients, auto-clickers, baritone pathfinders, and movement cheats will trigger instant automated bans.
Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, unsolicited advertisements, or excessive toxicity are strictly forbidden across both in-game chat and our Discord server. Keep the vibe constructive and supportive.
PvP is mutually consensual. Both players must agree before initiating combat unless inside designated PvP arenas or during organized community tournament events. Trap-killing inside Nether portals is strictly forbidden.
Large mob grinders, sorting facilities, and flying machines must feature an easily accessible manual on/off shutoff switch. Never leave high-frequency redstone clocks running while unattended or offline.
Maintain a courteous distance of at least 150 blocks from other players' established settlements unless you have explicitly coordinated a shared village or town. Avoid leaving floating trees or unfinished pillar towers.
